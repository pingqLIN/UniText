# Claude Design Families

Use this reference when the job is to choose one visual family before authoring or applying a `DESIGN.md`.

## Editorial Minimalism

- Best for: calm SaaS, docs-heavy products, pricing pages, operator tools
- Signals: neutral palette, strict type hierarchy, one accent, flat surfaces
- Strengths: trust, clarity, information density without noise
- Avoid when: the product needs theatrical motion, a playful consumer tone, or overt warmth
- Reference anchors: Linear, Stripe, Vercel, Mintlify

## Terminal-Core

- Best for: developer tools, infra products, CLI-adjacent brands, command surfaces
- Signals: monospace bias, hard edges, dark surfaces, phosphor or amber accents
- Strengths: operator credibility, speed, tool-like focus
- Avoid when: the product is mass-market consumer software or emotionally warm
- Reference anchors: Ollama, Warp, Raycast, OpenCode

## Warm Editorial

- Best for: thoughtful AI products, long-form content, premium note apps, research-led brands
- Signals: cream or paper backgrounds, serif display, humanist sans body, terracotta or clay accents
- Strengths: craft, trust, patience, literary tone
- Avoid when: the product must feel machine-fast, highly technical, or dashboard-dense
- Reference anchors: Claude, Notion, Resend, Substack

## Data-Dense Pro

- Best for: analytics, observability, financial or operations dashboards
- Signals: tight spacing, chart-first composition, fixed-width numerals, dark-first surfaces
- Strengths: throughput, scanability, operator confidence
- Avoid when: the main job is storytelling or marketing instead of rapid scanning
- Reference anchors: ClickHouse, PostHog, Grafana, Sentry, Supabase

## Cinematic Dark

- Best for: creator tools, AI media products, launch pages, visual storytelling
- Signals: oversized type, dark surfaces, saturated accent light, dramatic hero treatment
- Strengths: excitement, motion, campaign energy
- Avoid when: the product depends on sober trust or dense day-to-day productivity
- Reference anchors: Runway, ElevenLabs, Minimax, Midjourney

## Playful Color

- Best for: consumer tools, education, collaborative software, expressive brands
- Signals: high saturation, illustrated accents, rounded forms, shaped backgrounds
- Strengths: friendliness, memorability, approachability
- Avoid when: the product must feel restrained, severe, or institutional
- Reference anchors: Figma, Clay, Duolingo, Mailchimp, Cal.com

## Glass / Soft-Futurism

- Best for: premium consumer software, polished launch pages, soft-gradient brands
- Signals: translucency, blur, layered surfaces, pastel or mist gradients
- Strengths: premium softness, aspirational feel, polished consumer appeal
- Avoid when: the system must prioritize dense operational clarity or brutal simplicity
- Reference anchors: Apple, Arc, Airbnb, Granola, Spotify

## Neon Brutalist

- Best for: editorial statements, campaign pages, culture-forward products, high-attitude brands
- Signals: hard edges, strong hue commitment, deliberate contrast, typography with bite
- Strengths: courage, memorability, unmistakable identity
- Avoid when: the product needs to disappear behind usability or feel familiar and safe
- Reference anchors: Bugatti, PlayStation, The Verge, Pitchfork

## Cult / Indie

- Best for: smaller software brands, research labs, magazines, creative tools, non-enterprise products
- Signals: opinionated curation, warmth or darkness with niche references, lower template reuse
- Strengths: specificity, personality, non-corporate feel
- Avoid when: a procurement-friendly or highly standardized enterprise surface is required
- Reference anchors: Granola, Criterion, A24, Letterboxd, ProPublica, Obsidian

## Picking Heuristics

- Choose one family, not a committee of families.
- Read-heavy + trust-heavy usually points toward `Warm Editorial` or `Editorial Minimalism`.
- Scan-heavy + operator-heavy usually points toward `Data-Dense Pro` or `Terminal-Core`.
- Courage-heavy launch work usually points toward `Cinematic Dark`, `Neon Brutalist`, or `Glass / Soft-Futurism`.
- Consumer-friendly onboarding usually points toward `Playful Color`.
